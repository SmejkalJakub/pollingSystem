## Phase 4 continuation decisions

- Q02 authority confirmed: use [H21 guidelines](https://www.ih21.org/en/guidelines). Its accessible single-winner example and finite-candidate threshold conflict for three candidates, and its rounding notation is unexplained. Formula/table images were blocked by browser security; do not bypass the block. The 2026-10-17 decision below replaces the need for an automatic positive-limit formula in Phase 4. The user explicitly confirmed that each negative requires two positives actually cast: negatives used ≤ floor(positives used / 2); 0–1 positives permit zero negatives, 2–3 permit one, 4–5 permit two. Positive/negative maxima are explicitly entered by the administrator under the 2026-10-17 approval.
- Q09 confirmed/implemented: shared event teams and voting-specific entries, propagated descriptive corrections, draft-only entry edits, no removal/archive.
- Q05 reopening confirmed: admins may always reopen closed/archived voting without readiness checks, preserving ballots, frozen configuration and sticky roster lock. Active retries are idempotent; drafts still require first activation. API/UI are implemented. The user subsequently approved frozen voting configuration/entries/permissions after first activation. Shared team descriptive corrections remain allowed.

## Phase 4 completion decisions — 2026-10-17

User selected “Approve this setup”: explicit admin-entered positive/negative maxima, no automatic formula/rounding; selection count means advancing slots without a reversed elimination mode; voting rules/entries/T-V permissions frozen after first activation. H21 general conditions require more positives than selected places and combined positive/negative allowance below candidate count. Stored negative allowance cannot exceed half the positive allowance and is zero when disabled. Actual ballot eligibility remains one negative per two positives actually cast. No requirement to use all votes.

These decisions resolve Phase 4 Q02/Q05 gates and supersede earlier pending/proposed wording. The D21 interaction engine remains Phase 6. Automatic formulas, results/ties, submission/unlocking and deadlines are not introduced. Full Phase 4 validation is in progress.md.
